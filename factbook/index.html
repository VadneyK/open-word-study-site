New Testament Factbook

The people, places and things the New Testament names, each with a short description, family links, and every verse where the Greek text names them.

    A free study aid from open data. People, places, descriptions and relations: STEPBible TIPNR (CC BY 4.0, Tyndale House Cambridge). Mentions are words in the NA28 Greek text tagged with that individual in STEPBible TAGNT, so "he" and "him" are not counted, and a name used for more than one person is counted for the right one where STEPBible has told them apart. The place plot shows relative positions from TIPNR's coordinates; it is not a map. Not affiliated with any Bible software company.
