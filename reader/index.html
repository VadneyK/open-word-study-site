Interlinear Reader

Read the Bible in English with the Greek or Hebrew word under each English word. No Greek or Hebrew needed: tap a word to see what stands behind it.

Tap any word to see the word behind it.

A free study aid from open data and a work in progress. English, Greek, Hebrew and word alignment: Berean Standard Bible and the Berean interlinear tables (public domain). The Greek is the tables' Nestle base text and the Hebrew is the Westminster Leningrad Codex text they use. Word order follows the English, so the Greek or Hebrew words are not in their original order, and words with no English of their own are left out. This is a reading aid, not a replacement for learning the languages or for a full Bible software library. Not affiliated with any Bible software company.
