Open Word Study

Free tools for studying the words of the Bible in Greek and Hebrew. No account and no cost, built only from open data.

Try one: σκηνόω, "to dwell", in its 5 New Testament places, or δικαιοσύνη, "righteousness", in Matthew.

  • Word StudyAny Greek word: where it appears, its forms, how open translations render it, and words translated the same way.
  • Hebrew Word StudyThe same for every word of the Old Testament. Start with חֶסֶד, "loving devotion".
  • Word WheelOne word, every occurrence, a ring per translation. Start with σκηνόω, "to dwell".
  • Passage WorkbenchA passage word by word: rare words, repeated words, and where Greek editions differ.
  • Passage CompanionRead with the margin open: cross references readers found helpful, notes on hard phrases, people and places.
  • Grammar LensColor the commands, the aorists, or one word through a passage to see its shape.
  • Verse FinderVerses that meet several conditions at once: words, people, places and grammar.
  • FactbookPeople, places and things the New Testament names, with every mention.
