What was changed

The data was reshaped to build these tools. Renderings are grouped by their main English word, translation notes were lightly cleaned of formatting, and counts were computed from the tagged words. The parts of this site derived from the unfoldingWord texts and notes are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Greek counts use the words STEPBible's TAGNT marks as NA28 readings. The Nestle-Aland edition's publisher did not provide or review this data. Mistakes in how the data is combined here are this site's own.

What is not used

Copyrighted translations such as the ESV and NIV are not used. No data, text or interface from any commercial Bible software is used. These tools are a study aid, not a replacement for a full Bible software library.

Privacy

No accounts and no analytics. Your browser remembers the last word you opened, on your device only. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google can see that request; if they are blocked, the pages still work with your device's fonts.
