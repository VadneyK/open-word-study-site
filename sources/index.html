Openly licensed data

  • TAGNT, Translators Amalgamated Greek New Testament

    STEPBible.org, based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    Greek words, lemmas, Strong's numbers, morphology, glosses. Used in Word Study, Word Wheel, Passage Workbench, Grammar Lens, Verse Finder, Factbook and Compare Translations.

  • TAHOT, Translators Amalgamated Hebrew Old Testament

    STEPBible.org, based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    Hebrew words, lemmas, glosses, morphology. Used in Hebrew Word Study, Word Wheel and Grammar Lens. Its parsing codes are read with the OpenScriptures Hebrew morphology code table and STEPBible's own additions to it (TEHMC, CC BY 4.0).

  • TIPNR, Translators Individualised Proper Names with all References

    STEPBible.org, based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    People, places and things, with their names and references. Used in Factbook, Verse Finder and Passage Companion.

  • Cross references

    OpenBible.info. CC BY 4.0. Source.

    Cross references ranked by reader votes. Used in Passage Companion.

  • unfoldingWord Literal Text and Simplified Text

    unfoldingWord. CC BY-SA 4.0. ULT source, UST source.

    English renderings aligned to the Greek. Used in Word Study, Word Wheel, Passage Workbench and Compare Translations.

  • unfoldingWord Translation Notes

    unfoldingWord. CC BY-SA 4.0. Source.

    Notes on hard phrases, written to help translators. Used in Passage Companion.

What was changed

The data was reshaped to build these tools. Renderings are grouped by their main English word, translation notes were lightly cleaned of formatting, and counts were computed from the tagged words. The parts of this site derived from the unfoldingWord texts and notes are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Greek counts use the words STEPBible's TAGNT marks as NA28 readings. The Nestle-Aland edition's publisher did not provide or review this data. Mistakes in how the data is combined here are this site's own.

What is not used

Copyrighted translations such as the ESV and NIV are not used. No data, text or interface from any commercial Bible software is used. These tools are a study aid, not a replacement for a full Bible software library.

Privacy

No accounts and no analytics. Your browser remembers the last word or passage you opened, your display choices, and whether you turned on Larger text, on your device only. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google can see that request; if they are blocked, the pages still work with your device's fonts.
